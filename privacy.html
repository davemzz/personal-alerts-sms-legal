1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled for the David Mainz Personal Alerts SMS messaging program. This program sends automated informational text messages (for example personal finance balance or available-credit alerts and similar status notifications) to a small set of phone numbers that have expressly opted in — typically the operator and household members such as a spouse.

2. Information we collect

In connection with this SMS program, we may collect and process:

  • Mobile phone numbers used to send or receive program messages
  • Messaging consent / opt-in status and opt-out (STOP) requests
  • Message content and delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery status) generated by the messaging provider (Twilio)
  • Support requests when you reply HELP or email the contact address above

3. How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Deliver the informational SMS alerts you consented to receive
  • Honor opt-out and help requests
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and secure the messaging program
  • Meet carrier and legal compliance requirements for A2P messaging

Message frequency varies based on configured alerts and account activity. Typical volume is low (often a few messages per day or fewer). Message and data rates may apply.

4. Mobile information — no third-party marketing sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile numbers and related messaging data are used only to operate this personal alerts program. Service providers that process messages on our behalf (such as Twilio and mobile carriers) receive only what is needed to deliver SMS and comply with applicable rules; they are not authorized to use your number for their own marketing.

5. Data retention

Phone numbers and consent records are retained while the messaging program is active and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for compliance, troubleshooting, and dispute resolution. Delivery logs may be retained according to our messaging provider’s retention settings.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect messaging-related information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any program message. For help, reply HELP or email davemainz@yahoo.com.

8. Children’s privacy

This program is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the SMS program after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: davemainz@yahoo.com.
